# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

In short: respect the purpose of the project, engage kindly and honestly, respect different viewpoints, give and take
feedback gracefully, and take responsibility for your actions, including repairing harm when it happens. Harassment,
character attacks, discrimination, sexualization, sharing someone's private information and threats are not tolerated
in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@hikmethankolay](https://github.com/hikmethankolay),
privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer works to address and repair the harm,
and may warn, remove content from or block participants who break these rules.
